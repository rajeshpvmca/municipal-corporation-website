Historical Legacy

From Victorian Borough to Smart Metropolis

First incorporated by Royal Charter in 1888, Metropolitan Civic Corporation was established to unify sanitary inspection, clean municipal drinking water, and road paving across the metropolitan boroughs.

Through the industrial boom of the early 20th century, post-war reconstruction, and the digital revolution, our municipal council has evolved continuously. Today, our corporation combines deep historic heritage with ultra-modern smart city cloud governance, managing 48 thriving administrative wards.

The Civic Motto: "Salus Populi Suprema Lex"
"The Health and Welfare of the Public shall be the Supreme Law."

Organizational Matrix

Municipal Directorates & Wings

Six dedicated departments coordinating seamlessly under the Municipal Commissioner.

1. Civil Engineering & Roads

Oversees highway maintenance, storm drains, bridges, flyovers, and public street furniture.

2. Water & Sewerage Board

Manages 240 MLD water treatment plants, sewage networks, water pipelines, and quality testing.

3. Revenue & Council Assessment

Administers property tax assessments, commercial trade licensing, and municipal land registries.

4. Public Health & Sanitation

Conducts daily solid waste clearance, vector control, food hygiene audits, and immunization clinics.

5. Town & Country Planning

Directs master zoning plans, building plan approvals, heritage conservation, and housing bylaws.

6. Smart City & Innovation

Drives digital e-governance, GIS land mapping, automated IoT sensors, and citizen mobile applications.
