Mayoral Leadership

The Role of the Worshipful Mayor

The Mayor serves as the ceremonial head and lead democratic representative of the metropolitan populace, presiding over full council debates, representing the corporation at state functions, and championing civic welfare funds.

Working in partnership with the Leader of the Council and cross-party committee chairs, the Mayor's office ensures that council resolutions reflect community priorities while upholding the historic Royal Charter.

Checks & Balances

Standing Scrutiny Committees

Specialized committees conduct in-depth hearings, audit department expenditures, and review citizen petitions.

1. Planning & Highways Committee

Reviews large-scale commercial developments, urban master plans, and major road widening projects.

Meets: 1st Tuesday Monthly
2. Finance & Audit Committee

Conducts quarterly financial audits, examines procurement bids, and monitors municipal bond issuances.

Meets: 2nd Wednesday Monthly
3. Health & Social Welfare

Monitors food safety enforcement, adult social care funding, and public immunization programs.

Meets: 3rd Thursday Monthly
Citizen Voice

Participate in Council Assembly

Citizens residing in any of our 48 wards possess the statutory right to present petitions, submit written public questions, or speak during public comment intervals.

  • Petitions reaching 500 verified signatures trigger formal committee debate
  • All assemblies held in Council Chamber, City Hall and live webcast
  • Sign-language interpretation and hearing loops available

Integrity Standards

Standards of Public Life & Ethics

All elected members and municipal executives are bound by the Nolan Principles of Public Life: Selflessness, Integrity, Objectivity, Accountability, Openness, Honesty, and Leadership.

Our public register of councillors' pecuniary interests, gifts, and hospitality declarations is updated quarterly and published openly for public audit.
